## Recommendation

Primary target: **Artificial Life**.

Reason: ZUSE is best framed as artificial-life methodology plus mechanistic analysis of emergent computation in cellular automata. The current 59-page manuscript can plausibly enter as a journal article without radical surgery.

Fallback target: **Complex Systems**.

Reason: it is directly aligned with cellular automata, formal systems, and broad complex-systems readership. It is a strong second target if *Artificial Life* rejects or requests a scope shift.

Do not use ALIFE conference as the first target for the current manuscript. An 8-page conference version would be a different paper: a condensed story around the T=15/ANF mechanism or the ZUSE pipeline, not the full audit trail.

## Positioning by Venue

### Artificial Life

Frame ZUSE as a reproducible artificial-life discovery instrument. Emphasize:

- deterministic discovery loop;
- 20-world atlas;
- fragility and observer-artifact separation;
- periodic-background mechanism discovery;
- T=15/ANF gradient;
- falsification chain and negative results.

Avoid overselling autonomy. Keep the phrase: "not a universal autonomous scientist."

### Complex Systems

Frame the paper as a computational study of simple-component systems with complex overall behavior. Emphasize:

- ECA mechanisms;
- periodic backgrounds;
- oscillator/glider structure;
- algebraic normal form gradients;
- reproducibility.

Tone should be less "AI discovery" and more "empirical law discovery in cellular automata."

### Physica D

Only use if reframing toward nonlinear dynamics. Emphasize:

- mechanism-level dynamics;
- pattern formation on periodic backgrounds;
- causal cones;
- algebraic gradients as measurable nonlinear structure.

Weakness: ZUSE-as-pipeline may feel less central to the journal.

### ALIFE Conference

Use only for a companion paper. Candidate short-paper topics:

- "A reproducible agent pipeline for empirical law discovery in ECA";
- "A T=15 locking mechanism and ANF gradient in elementary cellular automata";
- "Negative results as mechanism filters in artificial-life discovery."

The current full manuscript should not be compressed directly to a conference submission without choosing one narrow thesis.
