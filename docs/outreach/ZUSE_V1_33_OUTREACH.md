# ZUSE v1.33 Outreach Pack

## Short release post

ZUSE v1.33 is out.

The new release extends the ANF-gradient audit beyond the original `rule_109`
catalog. A primitive length-8 `rule_73` holdout yields nine natural-period
`T=12` witnesses. Three remain comparable at a predeclared neighboring
horizon, while none of 27 control measurements becomes positive.

The result is real but bounded: robustness is partial and
background-conditioned, not a universal ECA law.
